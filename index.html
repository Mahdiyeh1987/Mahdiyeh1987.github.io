PhD Dissertation

University of Bologna · 2026 · PhD in Architecture and Design Cultures

Predicting Solar Potentials Using Machine Learning: A Data-Driven Approach to Map Solar Energy Potential in the Urban Fabric. The Case of Bologna

This doctoral research develops an interpretable, open-data machine-learning framework for mapping annual rooftop solar irradiation across Bologna, Italy. Integrating GIS-derived urban morphology, solar exposure indicators and LightGBM, the study evaluates prediction accuracy, spatial transferability and explainability to support rooftop photovoltaic screening and evidence-based urban energy planning.
